ASG Annual General Meeting 2026 Announced

Published: October 2026  |  Category: Governance & Society Events

The Governing Council of the Architectural Society of Ghana (ASG) formally announces that the 2026 Annual General Meeting (AGM) will take place on Saturday, 21 November 2026, commencing at 9:00 AM GMT at the Society Secretariat, No. 9 Atsuru Street, Accra.

Meeting Agenda & Key Business

The AGM serves as the principal constitutional forum for all categories of ASG membership. Key agenda items for the 2026 assembly include:

  • Presentation and adoption of the 2025/2026 President’s Annual Report
  • Review and auditing of the Society’s financial statements for the fiscal year
  • Formal ratification and adoption of the newly amended ASG Constitution
  • Elections for the 2027–2029 Executive Steering Committee and Regional Branch Representatives
  • Induction ceremony for newly chartered corporate members and candidate architects

Attendance & Delegate Accreditation

All chartered members, probationary candidates, students, and corporate partners in good financial standing are eligible to participate. Members can complete pre-registration through the member portal or in person at the Secretariat desk on arrival.
