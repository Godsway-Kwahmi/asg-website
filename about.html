About ASG

A community of professionals moving Ghanaian architecture forward.

Who we are

The Architectural Society of Ghana brings architects, designers and students together to raise the standard of practice and share what we learn.

Members work across housing, public buildings, heritage and planning, from studios in Accra to sites across the country.

Standards

We help members practise to a shared professional standard, from building codes to ethics.

Learning

Regular CPD sessions keep members current on technology, materials and regulation.

Community

Job posts, events and mentors connect students and firms with each other.
