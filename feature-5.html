Studio Kwame Architecture — Earth Innovation, Timber Engineering & Circular Design.

Emerging Studio: Studio Kwame Architecture

Studios: Kumasi & Accra  |  Practice Type: Compressed Earth Masonry, Timber Innovation & Bio-Based Design

Studio Kwame Architecture represents an ambitious vanguard of young Ghanaian practitioners championing carbon neutrality, localized material extraction, and vernacular tectonic innovation. Rooted in research originating at the Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), the studio merges traditional craftsmanship with contemporary computational design.

Compressed Stabilized Earth & Local Timber

By substituting carbon-heavy Portland cement masonry with locally pressed Compressed Stabilized Earth Blocks (CSEB) and sustainably harvested Ghanaian hardwoods (such as Dahoma and Odum), Studio Kwame significantly reduces embodied carbon in residential and institutional construction. The natural thermal mass of earth blocks stabilizes interior temperatures, removing the need for mechanical air-conditioning during peak midday heat.

Research & Built Works

The Earth School

A community primary school in the Ashanti Region built with stabilized earth blocks, vaulted clay tile roofs, and natural daylight funnels.

Timber Micro-Home

A modular, prefabricated wooden residential prototype designed for rapid assembly on uneven terrain with zero soil disruption.

Agro-Waste Bio-Insulation

Applied research turning agricultural by-products (coconut coir and rice husks) into acoustic and thermal building insulation.
