ASG Annual General Meeting 2026 Announced

Published: October 2026  |  Category: Governance & Society Events

The Governing Council of the Architectural Society of Ghana (ASG) formally announces that the 2026 Annual General Meeting (AGM) will convene on Saturday, 21 November 2026, at the Society Secretariat, No. 9 Atsuru Street, Accra.

The AGM agenda includes the presentation of the Annual Report, ratification of audited financial statements, elections for the 2027–2029 Executive Steering Committee, and the formal adoption of the updated ASG Constitution. All chartered, candidate, student, and corporate members in good standing are urged to attend. Member credentials will be validated at the door.

Updated ASG Constitution

Published: September 2026  |  Category: Regulation & Governance

The Society’s constitution has been updated following extensive nationwide consultations with members, academic partners, and legal advisors. The revisions introduce modernized governance structures, updated digital voting procedures, formalized continuous professional development (CPD) mandates, and a refreshed Code of Professional Conduct.

Copies of the complete ratified document are available in the ASG Showroom and will be submitted for final member endorsement at the upcoming AGM on 21 November 2026.

Ghana Building Code Revision Workshop

Published: August 2026  |  Category: Technical Standards & CPD

The ASG Technical and Practice Committee, in collaboration with the Ministry of Works and Housing and the Ghana Standards Authority, will host an intensive consultative workshop on the forthcoming updates to the Ghana National Building Regulations (LI 1630).

The workshop will examine newly developed sections regarding seismic design specifications, energy efficiency metrics for tropical climates, universal accessibility standards, and fire protection engineering in high-density urban developments. Registered participants earn 4 accredited CPD credits.

Young Architects Mentorship Programme Launch

Published: July 2026  |  Category: Education & Early Career

ASG is proud to launch the 2026/2027 Young Architects Mentorship Programme, structured to bridge the transition from architectural graduation to professional chartered licensure. The programme pairs early-career practitioners and recent graduates from KNUST, Central University, and other accredited institutions with seasoned chartered architects.

Mentees will participate in one-on-one portfolio reviews, site supervision walkthroughs, and contract administration clinics over a structured 12-month period.

New International Accreditations & Partnerships

Published: June 2026  |  Category: International Affairs

The Architectural Society of Ghana has concluded bilateral recognition and collaboration agreements with the Commonwealth Association of Architects (CAA) and the African Union of Architects (AUA). Under these historic accords, ASG members gain access to international professional directories, cross-border CPD courses, and reciprocal licensing frameworks across participating Commonwealth and Pan-African jurisdictions.

These partnerships highlight the global standard of Ghanaian architectural education and practice.
