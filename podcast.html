Featured Episode • Season 2, Ep. 6

Bioclimatic Design & Passive Architecture in West Africa

Guests: Martin & Franziska Orthner (Orthner Orthner Associates)  |  Host: Architectural Society of Ghana  |  Duration: 48 mins

In this episode, we sit down with the founders of OOA in Accra to explore how bioclimatic architecture translates into practical, scalable construction in Ghana. From passive solar shading and natural cross-ventilation to rainwater harvesting and reducing operational carbon in institutional buildings, we examine the future of climate-responsive African architecture.

About The ASG Podcast

The Architectural Society of Ghana Podcast is an ongoing audio documentary and interview series bringing together leading practitioners, urban theorists, structural engineers, historians, and emerging design voices. Every fortnight, we unpack the ideas, technologies, and policies shaping Ghana's architectural landscape.

Whether you are a practising architect logging CPD hours, a design student, or an urbanist passionate about African cities, our episodes offer deep technical insight and critical cultural dialogue.
