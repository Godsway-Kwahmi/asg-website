Firm Highlight 1: Orthner Orthner Associates (OOA)

Accra, Ghana  |  Bioclimatic Architecture & Ecological Masterplanning

Orthner Orthner Associates (OOA) is widely celebrated across West Africa for its pioneering dedication to sustainable, climate-responsive design. Operating from their self-sufficient, solar-powered studio in Accra, OOA demonstrates how modern architecture in Ghana can achieve thermal comfort with minimal environmental footprint through passive solar shading, cross-ventilation, and rainwater harvesting.

Their portfolio spans educational campuses, healthcare facilities, and low-energy office complexes that integrate local natural materials with contemporary environmental engineering, establishing new standards for sustainable institutional, commercial, and residential developments across Ghana.

Project Feature 2: National Heritage Conservation

Accra, Cape Coast & Kumasi  |  Adaptive Reuse, Civic Heritage & Vernacular Conservation

The National Heritage Conservation initiative is an interdisciplinary effort championed by Ghanaian architects and conservationists to protect, document, and revitalize Ghana’s rich architectural legacy. From the mid-century modern civic landmarks constructed in post-independence Accra to the centuries-old Asante traditional buildings and coastal heritage fortresses, this project demonstrates how adaptive reuse safeguards cultural identity while fostering public engagement.

Using 3D laser scanning, archival research, and community-led preservation strategies, the initiative provides a comprehensive methodology for sustainable restoration, ensuring historic structures remain active centers of public life.

Practice Focus 3: Molders Design & Build

Greater Accra  |  Contemporary Commercial Architecture & Construction Engineering

Molders Design & Build brings rigorous structural precision and contemporary architectural aesthetics to Ghana’s rapidly evolving urban skyline. As a strategic partner of the Architectural Society of Ghana, Molders integrates architectural design with high-performance construction management, delivering landmark commercial developments, retail centers, and industrial facilities.

Their design philosophy balances operational efficiency, advanced building envelopes, and durable material palettes engineered specifically to withstand the tropical coastal climate of Accra.

Firm Highlight 4: Made in Accra

Accra, Ghana  |  Urban Design, Public Space & Community Architecture

Made in Accra is an innovative design collective and architectural practice moving Ghanaian architecture forward through contextual urban design, placemaking, and community-centric interventions. Their work focuses on how public spaces, pedestrian walkways, and mixed-use cultural nodes can celebrate Accra’s vibrant street life and historical neighborhoods.

Through participatory design workshops, contextual material choices, and climate-conscious urban typologies, Made in Accra transforms civic spaces into inclusive environments for work, gathering, and cultural expression.

Emerging Studio 5: Studio Kwame Architecture

Kumasi & Accra  |  Compressed Earth Blocks, Timber Innovation & Circular Design

Representing the next generation of Ghanaian architectural practice, Studio Kwame explores the intersection of indigenous African materiality and contemporary digital design. Specializing in compressed stabilized earth blocks (CSEB), engineered local timber, and passive cooling courtyards, the studio delivers climate-resilient residential, educational, and cultural projects.

Their research-driven practice minimizes embodied carbon while reviving traditional artisan crafts and empowering regional building supply chains.
