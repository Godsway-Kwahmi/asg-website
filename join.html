Join Us

Membership policy, categories, benefits and application process.

Application Process

Joining the Architectural Society of Ghana follows three structured steps from initial application to formal induction:

  1. Request & Complete Forms. Submit your initial details through the online registration form or request the full membership application pack via the website.
  2. Committee Review. The Membership Committee reviews your verified qualifications and documentation, then makes a recommendation to the Executive Council for approval.
  3. Approval & Membership Oath. Upon approval, members formally sign off on the ASG Membership Oath and endorse the professional Code of Conduct.

Membership Benefits

The Society provides comprehensive professional development, advocacy, networking, and industry privileges across six thematic areas:

1. Professional Development

  • Continuous professional development (CPD) programs (seminars, workshops, masterclasses) in partnership with GhIE, GIA, RIBA, and ISDCMG.
  • Access to publications, newsletters, reports, research journals, and technical resources.
  • Knowledge-sharing platforms for best practices, innovations, and case studies.

2. Networking & Collaboration

  • Opportunities to connect with fellow architects, allied professionals, and policymakers.
  • Mentorship programs dedicated to students and emerging professionals.
  • Participation in conferences, exhibitions, competitions, and curated design forums.
  • Special interest groups in sustainability, heritage, urbanism, and digital tools.

3. Advocacy & Representation

  • A collective voice on policy, legislation, and regulations affecting architects.
  • Direct representation to government, regulatory bodies, and international partners.
  • Recognition of professional excellence through Society awards and honours.

4. Career Support & Advancement

  • Mentorship programs linking emerging architects with experienced professionals.
  • Exclusive access to job boards, project opportunities, and architectural competitions.
  • Guidance for licensure, registration, and lifelong career progression.

5. Member Privileges

  • Access to negotiated welfare benefits (travel, professional indemnity insurance, health, pensions).
  • Discounts on training, conferences, and Society publications.
  • Industry partnerships for reduced rates on software, books, and professional tools.
  • Official barcoded stamps for practice documentation.

6. Community & Social Impact

  • Engagement in public outreach and built-environment advocacy campaigns.
  • Participation in community architecture and civic design projects.
  • Active promotion of sustainability, cultural heritage, and social responsibility.

Code of Conduct

Members, in addition to the statutory code of conduct set up by the Architects Act, adhere strictly to this professional code of conduct aimed at enhancing and promoting the interests of members, clients, and the general public. Members sign off on this code upon admission:

1. Professional Integrity
  • Uphold honesty, fairness, and transparency in all professional dealings.
  • Avoid misrepresentation of qualifications, experience, or work.
  • Refrain from any action that discredits the Society or the profession.
2. Competence & Responsibility
  • Accept and undertake only those tasks for which one is qualified.
  • Continuously seek to improve professional knowledge and skills.
  • Deliver work with due care, diligence, and in accordance with accepted standards.
3. Respect & Fair Practice
  • Treat clients, colleagues, and the public with respect and dignity.
  • Avoid conflicts of interest or disclose them promptly when they arise.
  • Ensure fairness in collaborations, competitions, and professional opportunities.
4. Confidentiality
  • Protect confidential information obtained through professional work.
  • Avoid unauthorized use or disclosure of client or Society information.
5. Ethical Business Practice
  • Charge fair, transparent, and reasonable fees.
  • Avoid corrupt practices, bribery, or unfair competition.
  • Respect intellectual property rights and originality of work.
6. Commitment to Society
  • Promote architecture that contributes positively to public welfare, safety, and sustainability.
  • Advocate for environmentally responsible and socially inclusive design.
  • Avoid projects that violate human rights, environmental justice, or community well-being.
7. Loyalty to the Society
  • Adhere to the rules, policies, and decisions of the Society.
  • Represent the Society positively in public and professional forums.
  • Report unethical or unprofessional conduct of members in accordance with due process.

FAQ

Who can join?

Registered architects, architectural technologists, technical professionals, allied built-environment degree holders, and students enrolled in accredited architecture programs.

What are the membership categories?

ASG offers eight categories: Chartered Members (ASG), Associate Member (Assoc. ASG), Technical Members (Tech. ASG), Affiliate Members, Students, Fellows (F. ASG), Chartered Practice, and Honorary Members (Hon. ASG).

What does membership cost?

Student membership is free. Honorary membership is free with voluntary periodic contributions. Fees for Chartered, Associate, Technical, Affiliate, and Fellow members are determined by the Executive Council. Email info@asg.com for the current schedule.

How long does approval take?

The Membership Committee aims to review complete submissions and reply within two to three weeks of receiving an application.

Can I log CPD hours?

Yes. Attendance at listed ASG sessions, seminars, workshops, and lectures counts toward your professional CPD record.

What is required to become an ASG Fellow?

Fellowship requires at least 15 years of practice as a chartered architect and the delivery of an inaugural lecture on sustainable building design.
