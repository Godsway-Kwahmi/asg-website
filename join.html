Join Us

Membership for architects, graduates and students.

Process

  1. Apply. Fill in the registration form.
  2. Review. The membership committee checks your details.
  3. Confirm. We email you the outcome.
  4. Start. Log in, join events and log CPD.

FAQ

Who can join?

Registered architects, graduates and students of architecture.

What does it cost?

Fees depend on membership type. Email info@asg.com for the current schedule.

How long does approval take?

We aim to reply within a few weeks of receiving a complete application.

Can I log CPD hours?

Yes. Attendance at listed sessions counts towards your CPD record.
