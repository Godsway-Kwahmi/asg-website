Orthner Orthner Associates — Sustainable Bioclimatic Architecture in Ghana.

Firm Highlight: Orthner Orthner Associates (OOA)

Studio: Accra, Ghana  |  Practice Type: Bioclimatic Architecture & Environmental Urbanism

Orthner Orthner Associates (OOA) is an award-winning architectural firm committed to climate-responsive, sustainable design tailored to the tropical conditions of West Africa. Founded with a vision to redefine African contemporary architecture through environmental responsibility, the studio integrates passive design strategies, local resource utilization, and low-energy construction methodologies.

Bioclimatic Design & Energy Autonomy

Operating out of their self-designed, energy-autonomous studio in Accra, OOA serves as a living laboratory for sustainable architectural solutions. The building features an optimized building envelope with deep overhangs, sun-shading brise-soleil, natural ventilation funnels, and an integrated photovoltaic array that powers daily studio operations. Rainwater collection systems supply the building’s non-potable water needs, demonstrating zero-net energy concepts in practice.

Selected Projects

OOA Solar Studio

Zero-net operational energy design in central Accra utilizing passive solar orientation, exposed earthen textures, and local timber sunscreens.

Ecological Health Facility

A regional healthcare center in the Volta Region engineered with courtyard airflow microclimates and daylighting to enhance patient recovery.

Tropical Residential Typologies

A series of contemporary family residences across Greater Accra highlighting vernacular masonry and thermal mass cooling.
