Young Architects Mentorship Programme Launch

Published: July 2026  |  Category: Education & Early Career

The Architectural Society of Ghana proudly announces the official launch of the 2026/2027 Young Architects Mentorship Programme. Conceived to support early-career professionals bridging the demanding gap between university graduation and professional licensing, this flagship program pairs emerging architects with distinguished senior fellows across Ghana.

Program Structure & Benefits

Selected mentees will embark on a comprehensive 12-month structured curriculum, including:

  • One-on-One Mentorship: Monthly advisory sessions with experienced studio principals and project directors.
  • Site Supervision Clinics: Hands-on walkthroughs on active construction sites to master site inspections, quality control, and contractor coordination.
  • Contract & Practice Administration: In-depth workshops on client contracts, professional fee scales, planning permits, and public sector tenders.
  • Professional Portfolio Guidance: Targeted review and preparation for the professional practice exams (PPE).

Eligibility & Application Guidelines

Applications are open to candidate members, graduate architects, and final-year master's students from accredited architectural faculties. Learn more about candidacy on the Membership Policy & Process page.
