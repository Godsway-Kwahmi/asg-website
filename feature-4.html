Made in Accra — Moving Ghanaian Architecture Forward Through Urban Placemaking.

Firm Highlight: Made in Accra

Studio: Accra, Ghana  |  Practice Type: Urban Design, Civic Placemaking & Contextual Architecture

Made in Accra is an architecture and urbanism collective committed to moving Ghanaian architecture forward. Founded by a dynamic team of Ghanaian practitioners, the studio interrogates the informal complexities, energetic street life, and social fabrics of urban Accra to produce authentic, climate-resilient architecture.

Urban Typologies & Public Space Activation

Rather than imposing detached international models, Made in Accra draws directly upon Ghanaian social patterns — verandahs, communal compounds, shaded canopies, and marketplace walkways. Their work emphasizes the creation of inclusive public realms that encourage social cohesion, support local micro-economies, and celebrate civic life under the tropical sun.

Signature Initiatives

Accra Public Realm Masterplan

Strategic pedestrian frameworks and tree-canopied streets connecting key cultural hubs in Jamestown and Osu.

Community Arts Pavilion

An open-air civic pavilion constructed using recycled structural steel, modular bamboo lattices, and porous paving.

Urban Infill Housing

Contextual multi-family housing prototypes that provide high-density living without sacrificing traditional cross-ventilation.
