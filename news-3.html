Consultative workshop on national building code revisions and seismic standards.

Ghana Building Code Revision Workshop

Published: August 2026  |  Category: Technical Standards & CPD

The ASG Practice and Technical Standards Committee, in strategic partnership with the Ministry of Works and Housing and the Ghana Standards Authority (GSA), is organizing a two-day consultative technical workshop to review critical updates to the Ghana National Building Regulations (LI 1630).

Key Technical Tracks

With Accra and surrounding coastal zones designated as seismically active belts, updating building design codes is imperative for life safety and urban resilience. The workshop sessions will cover:

  • Seismic Engineering Provisions: Ductility, structural joints, and foundation tie-beams in multi-storey masonry and concrete frames.
  • Fire & Life Safety Engineering: Compartmentation, egress calculations, smoke evacuation in high-rise and mixed-use commercial centers.
  • Thermal Comfort & Energy Efficiency: Envelope transmittance (U-values), solar heat gain coefficients (SHGC), and natural ventilation minimums.
  • Universal Accessibility: Mandatory inclusion of barrier-free ramps, tactile paving, and accessible sanitary spaces in all public buildings.

All participating registered architects earn 4 formal ASG CPD units. Session schedules and registration details are available in the ASG Learn section.
