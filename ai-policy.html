AI Policy

How the Society uses and thinks about AI.

Our approach

ASG uses AI tools to support administration. We do not use them to decide membership or hiring.

For members

Members stay responsible for their professional judgement and should disclose AI use where clients require it.

Questions

Email info@asg.com.

Ask ASG

The assistant on this site is a search tool, not a language model. It matches your question to wording we have already published and links you to that page. It adds nothing, decides nothing and keeps nothing.

It has no access to applications, payments or member records, and it is never used to decide membership or hiring. Put anything about your own case to a person at info@asg.com.
