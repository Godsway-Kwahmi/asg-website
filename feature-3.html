Molders Design & Build — High-Performance Commercial Architecture & Construction.

Practice Focus: Molders Design & Build

Studio: Greater Accra  |  Practice Type: Integrated Design-Build, Commercial Architecture & Engineering

Molders Design & Build stands as one of Ghana's premier integrated architecture and construction management practices. Known for precision engineering, contemporary architectural vocabulary, and strict delivery standards, Molders has shaped key commercial, mixed-use, and industrial developments in Accra and across Ghana.

Integrated Design-Led Construction

By bringing architectural designers, structural engineers, and site managers under a single coordinated workflow, Molders eliminates costly misalignments between drafting and execution. Their projects feature advanced ventilated facades, high-specification acoustic glazing, and energy-efficient building management systems (BMS) adapted to the West African climate.

Key Commercial Milestones

Commercial Towers

Multi-storey corporate headquarters in Airport City featuring double-skin solar facades and efficient open-plan office layouts.

Logistics & Industrial Facilities

Wide-span steel warehouse facilities and light industrial parks in Tema engineered for logistical throughput and durability.

Mixed-Use Retail

Community commercial hubs that blend neighborhood retail amenities with professional co-working and public gathering terraces.
