Ratified revisions to Society governance and code of professional ethics.

Updated ASG Constitution

Published: September 2026  |  Category: Regulation & Governance

The Architectural Society of Ghana has published the comprehensive revised edition of its Constitution and Code of Conduct, concluding an 18-month consultative review with practicing architects, regional branches, and legal counsel.

Key Amendments & Policy Modernization

The updated constitutional framework introduces vital structural updates designed to meet 21st-century professional demands:

  • Digital Governance: Introduction of secure electronic voting and hybrid general assembly participation for diaspora and regional members.
  • Mandatory CPD Framework: Clear annual continuous professional development quotas (minimum 20 hours per annum) for practicing license renewals.
  • Streamlined Committee Oversight: Formalization of dedicated subcommittees on Practice Standards, Climate Action, Diversity & Inclusion, and Young Architects.
  • Enhanced Disciplinary Procedures: Transparent mechanisms for peer review, public complaints resolution, and professional sanctions.

The complete text is available for inspection at the Secretariat, accessible through the ASG Showroom, and outlined on the Membership Policy page.
