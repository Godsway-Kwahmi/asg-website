ASG concludes historic agreements with Commonwealth & Pan-African bodies.

New International Accreditations & Partnerships

Published: June 2026  |  Category: International Affairs & Professional Practice

The Architectural Society of Ghana is pleased to announce the successful signing of bilateral accreditation and mutual recognition pacts with the Commonwealth Association of Architects (CAA) and the African Union of Architects (AUA).

Global Recognition for Ghanaian Architects

These landmark agreements expand international opportunities and knowledge transfer for Ghanaian architects across Africa and globally:

  • Cross-Border Practice Portability: Streamlined professional recognition for ASG chartered members practicing in participating Commonwealth and African partner nations.
  • International CPD Courses: Free reciprocal access to accredited online masterclasses, seminars, and sustainability symposia hosted by partner institutes.
  • Global Student Exchange & Studio Residencies: Collaborative exchange opportunities for Ghanaian architectural students with leading design universities abroad.
  • Harmonized Climate Benchmarks: Alignment with international benchmarks for decarbonization and climate-resilient building envelopes in tropical latitudes.

For more details on international affiliations and partner organizations, visit the ASG Partners section.
